Rulebook

These rules are the baseline for fair play. Staff may clarify edge cases, but rules should be applied consistently.

1. Respect and conduct

Treat players respectfully. Harassment, hate speech, threats, targeted discrimination, doxxing and sustained disruptive behaviour are prohibited. Keep disagreements civil and use staff channels for disputes.

2. Fair play and exploits

No hacked clients, cheats, x-ray, duping, exploiting unintended mechanics, lag machines or abuse of bugs. Report exploits privately to staff. Do not use alternate accounts to evade bans or restrictions.

3. Builds and claims

Do not grief, steal, trespass in protected areas or build offensively near another player's property without permission. Respect agreed borders and public infrastructure. Resolve territorial disputes through diplomacy or the server's declared conflict process.

4. War and diplomacy

Wars must follow the server's current declaration, preparation and end-of-war procedures. No surprise attacks where prohibited, combat logging, exploiting safe zones or using prohibited tactics. Honour signed treaties unless they are lawfully ended under server rules.

5. Economy and trade

No scamming, duped goods, real-money trading unless expressly permitted, or exploiting market systems. Honour trade agreements and report suspicious transactions.

6. Names, skins and chat

Keep usernames, skins, signs and public chat appropriate. Do not impersonate staff or another player. Advertising unrelated servers or services requires permission.

7. Staff and appeals

Do not impersonate staff or interfere with moderation. If sanctioned, use the designated appeal process. Staff decisions should include a reason where practical; abuse of staff permissions can be reported to the owner.

8. Application integrity

Submit truthful information and one application per player. Do not spam applications or pressure reviewers. If denied, contact an administrator on the official Discord to request an application reset.

9. Rule updates

Rules may be updated as the server develops. The version published here is the reference version; major changes should be announced in Discord.
